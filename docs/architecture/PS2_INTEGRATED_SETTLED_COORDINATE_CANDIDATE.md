## Closure, ownership, and accounting

Each StateRoot inventory exactly equals its authored/history/operation/resource semantic closure. It excludes the StateRoot and its own inventory structural nodes. The global base contains all distinct selected StateRoot objects, their semantic closures, pin/evidence selectors, ConversionSource, accounting tree nodes and accounting records. The bounded overlay adds ledger and envelope.

**All inventory structural pages are excluded from global member entries in these new coordinates.** This is an explicit change from the historical joined specimen, which included role inventory pages in the global union. Inventory pages remain mandatory typed traversal edges: every StateRoot inventory and global base tree is independently traversed, located, authenticated, and owned. Ownership/locator/root-placement implementation records are also physically traversed/owned rather than self-inventoried. Their omission from a member list is not permission to omit their bytes. Per-role locator key equality covers exactly that role's semantic/state/inventory objects, complete selected global metadata dependencies, and ownership tree/claims. Unknown extra logical locator entries refuse.

Three independent per-root data/metadata pairs share one immutable receipt allocation and one metadata allocation holding the directly authenticated root-placement tree. Every role owns that shared root-placement allocation. Removing its claim, borrowing another role's placement, omitting a required resource locator, or substituting sealed versus growable classification refuses. A sealed claim must name the exact selected charge ObjectRef, not an equal-total substitute. Sealed prefixes cover every byte; current tips use an empty authenticated prefix plus authenticated referenced frames and exact full extent ownership. Unreachable and zero-padding bytes remain owned and charged. The proposed 16-byte `PS2PKD01` framing is inherited as an explicit unfrozen engineering choice; no permanent magic approval is implied. Padding is tag 0, all zero, bounded by the actual file, and cannot be referenced as an object.

The complete accounting result is **2,076,672 synthetic registered bytes**: seven 262,144-byte tips (1,835,008), one sealed receipt allocation charged 4,096, eighteen original snapshot files charged 73,728 individually by path, 131,072 standing controls, 16,384 pack/directory allowance, and 16,384 retained-snapshot directory allowance. Actual selected loose/HEAD/commit/manifest controls consume 28,672 rounded bytes of the standing pool. Empty or same-content files are not deduplicated by digest for charging. The two directory allowances are explicit injected local profile charges; this corpus does not establish a native filesystem allocation model or future transient-control admission envelope.

Full validation checks all actual supplied allocation/file bytes and synthetic original witnesses against selected observations. Same-byte inode substitution cannot inherit a recorded charge. `metadata` accounting validation checks parsed field/closure/aggregate consistency without fabricating physical evidence. The independent recovery entry uses that metadata check and separately compares every supplied recovery/shared allocation witness, extent, and sealed prefix. It reads recovery after active and retained-role private files are removed, but cannot establish the complete global semantic union, absent allocations' physical charge, write admission, settlement, or retirement eligibility. It returns a role proof, not a physically verified global accounting total.

Legacy authored records are intentionally restricted to the exact immutable operation specimen's supported bytes. The reader does not claim to implement every existing legacy authored schema by its abbreviated edge walker. ConversionSource is likewise compared with the exact original descriptor while its actual snapshot files are independently checked. No historical missing-source fallback is used.

## Bounds and remaining scope

The shared existing bounded canonical parser is used before accepting every referenced JSON body: 1 MiB per record, depth 64, 4,096 object members and array entries. The diagnostic fixture entry is bounded to 8 MiB before decoding; packed bytes total at most 16 MiB across at most 16 allocations, source bytes at most 16 MiB across at most 256 files, and loose input at most 2 MiB total with 1 MiB per body and 24 roles. The selected standing pool imposes a tighter actual loose-charge bound. Semantic trees have depth below 16, at most 512 visited nodes per traversal, leaves at most four and fanout two through four. Accounting leaves retain the component's three-entry limit; physical locator leaves at most 64, branches two through eight, depth below 16 and at most 256 nodes. Current tips are at most eight. These are explicit **disposable codec/profile test bounds**, not newly approved product retention, media-size, project-capacity, or permanent wire limits.

Seventeen tests pass, including fifteen coherently regenerated full-package negative variants. Those variants target original descriptor substitution, unsupported original history version, contradictory retained root UUIDs, borrowed pin placement, missing shared ownership, sealed-as-tip classification, alternate sealed charge references, undercharge, missing capability, invented resource origin, missing resource location, wrong resource membership, wrong history edge type, conflicting cross-root association identity, and missing global membership.
