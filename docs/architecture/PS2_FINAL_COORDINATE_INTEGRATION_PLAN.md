# PS2 final-coordinate integration plan

Status: **concrete engineering draft; unfrozen, unimplemented, not permanent-format approval**. This document fixes a proposed composition boundary so the next disposable reader can be built and reviewed. It does not rename historical `example.ps2` bytes or claim that the component proofs already compose. The source checkpoints are resource/accounting `0d07e79`, origin/newborn checklists `3e65f52`, and whole-Blob `fa6a2a4`.

## One selector and two different closures

Keep HEAD v1, outer commit v1, original bootstrap bytes, ObjectRef identity and operation receipt/digest meanings unchanged. HEAD alone chooses the exact commit. The commit embeds RootSet v2; its outer `authored`, `history`, and `inventory` still equal the active StateRoot fields. Required capabilities, reader floor and RootSet schema/discriminator must jointly dispatch the new reader before effects; exact new capability/floor values remain a reviewed integration output. Do not reinterpret RootSet v1, StateRoot v1, flat inventory v1 or operation-index v1.

The following field set is the proposed top-level contract. `JRef` means the unchanged JSON ObjectRef. A **typed edge** is traversed and included in the appropriate exact closure; UUIDs, hashes and provenance are not implicit edges. All records retain checked schema/project/extensions envelopes; package roots additionally retain library/bootstrap identity. Unknown mandatory fields/versions refuse; optional qualified extension values are preserved as nonedges.

StateRoot inventory contains only the typed closure of authored/history/resource-state/operation-index. It excludes that StateRoot and its own inventory structural nodes; it contains neither physical accounting nor a current phase. RootSet's global inventory is the exact union of all selected root objects, semantic inventories/closures, conversion metadata, retention evidence, and logical accounting/control dependencies. Inventory implementation nodes and the overlay itself are separately located, authenticated and physically owned; they do not inventory themselves. HEAD/commit/bootstrap and original conversion snapshot files retain their separate keep-set/accounting rules. This preserves the appendix's semantic distinction rather than making physical controls authored data.

The proposed global overlay is `photara.package.inventory-overlay` v1 `{base:JRef, controls:[JRef], count:Decimal}`: a packed typed union inventory plus the finite current loose-control difference, sorted, unique and disjoint from the base. The overlay excludes itself. `controls` is bounded by the **original admitted control envelope**, not a lifetime pin limit. Lifetime holds, evidence and pin membership use trees. Structural tree pages remain authenticated through their typed roots/physical references even though they are excluded from their own member set. The implementation must prove this exact closure partition; arbitrary omitted implementation records are not allowed. Shared root-placement tree pages themselves must be authenticated and physically owned. Their direct physical child commitments do not require a page or containing writable tip to include its own whole-file hash.

## Coordinate groups to compose, without changing component meanings

Use the exact proposed [factored resource coordinates](PS2_FACTORED_RESOURCE_BYTE_CANDIDATE.md), including the six typed ResourceState v2 selectors and immutable source-association identity. Origin contexts come from the actual selected roots and evidence, never caller-supplied accepted-ID lists. Proposed `photara.resource.retention-evidence-leaf/branch` v1 keys `(origin kind,source_id)` and selects explicit-policy or pending-operation records described in the [origin proposal](PS2_RETENTION_ORIGIN_EVIDENCE_PROPOSAL.md). Detailed policy/pending record fields must be instantiated and tested before this group is complete. Root-origin associations resolve selected root/role/history context. Five origin kinds do not replace the twelve operational pin classes or their release guards. Selected operational-pin records describe retained snapshots and reconciliation evidence; they do not turn ReaderLease handles, runtime access grants or local concurrency observations into portable authority.

Use operation-index v2 with separate typed operation-ID and ordinal trees, preserving receipt v1, accepted-prefix and journal-prefix domains. Use **new** inventory leaf/branch coordinates rather than changing flat `photara.package.inventory` v1. Use the [accounting candidate's](PS2_ACCOUNTING_SCALING_CANDIDATE.md) exact proposed observation, sealed-charge and retained-file-charge trees. The integrated ledger additionally selects bounded current-tip summaries, standing control/namespace/directory allowances, and typed retirement-ticket and operational-pin roots. These additions need exact byte schemas and linked vectors; the accounting subset is not already that ledger. Charge each physical allocation once while preserving every retention edge; retained original files remain separately charged and never implicitly released.

The [whole-Blob proposal](PS2_WHOLE_BLOB_BYTE_CANDIDATE.md) supplies raw whole-allocation placement and layout-specific claims; the combined locator/observation dispatcher must explicitly accept both framed JSON and whole-Blob variants. Do not silently reinterpret the untagged historical framed fixture or treat structural opening as a raw-media hash audit. Local observation scope/profile equality is not storage qualification. Portable resource/backing records do not acquire device/inode fields.

The final specimen needs one project-consistent HEAD. The exact original D19 Blob specimen has a different project from the Graph route. Keep its exact-byte preservation proof intact. A route-project managed Blob record may be generated as **new metadata**, or a separate genuine D19-derived whole-HEAD case may be constructed; rewriting D19 project IDs is not preservation of those original bytes.

## Original admission and measured finalization: the acyclic order

The frozen route's original `target.base_inventory` and full target placement cannot be reused unchanged for newborn generations: new sealed charges depend on measurements after payload writes. The new original admission binds the **semantic target**, original request/receipt identity, exact old HEAD/commit/ledger, precomputable payload plan, original reserve `R`, cleanup allowance, finite control limits, and the immutable generation/finalizer plan. It does not bind the future global inventory, future accounting envelope, or not-yet-measured sealed charges.

1. **Prepare, without effects.** Construct target semantic StateRoots/inventories, payload recipe and finite birth slots. Pending associations may name the original OperationId; their evidence referring to the admission is selected later outside this semantic target. Prove worst-case packed finalizer corridor and simultaneous rounded controls, staging/directory allowances and role count fit the original reserve and fixed project limit. No speculative source credit.
2. **Select the original hold.** HEAD selects the same immutable original admission and initial phase under the old semantic roots. Its typed `generation_plan` edge selects proposed `photara.storage.generation-plan` v1. The plan has token/request/old-base commitments but no back edge to the original that contains it. Reserve precedes marker creation, journal acceptance and payload writes. The admission's changed holds-tree path must be representable as bounded loose records selected through the overlay, within the existing standing control pool, before any packed effects. Writing a packed holds-tree path first and then calling its admission a reservation would recreate the reserve-before-effects cycle.
3. **Enroll and replay.** Selected phase edges `newborn_binding` and later `finalization` use proposed `photara.storage.newborn-binding` and `photara.storage.finalization-control` v1. Marker/binding rules, exact inode identity, partial-stage fences, no-replace promotion and original-token retention follow the [newborn checklist](PS2_NEWBORN_REPLAY_INTEGRATION_CHECKLIST.md). Replay only the admitted exact payload on witnessed allocations. Verify original prefixes and complete suffixes, including unreachable frames. The original R never expands.
4. **Measure, then select the finalizer before its writes.** After payload durability barriers, observe actual newly sealed extents/highwaters. The immutable finalizer embeds their exact canonical observation/charge bodies and an exact regenerable finalization recipe using the originally reserved final tips/corridor. These bodies need not already exist as packed objects. The selected phase authenticates this finalizer while semantic roots still remain old. No finalizer edge points to its containing phase/envelope/overlay. Detailed fields are delegated to [the newborn original-field proposal](PS2_NEWBORN_ORIGINAL_FIELD_PROPOSAL.md).
5. **Write finalization and publish atomically.** Emit those exact measured sealed records outside their own sealed allocation, plus physical trees/global base membership. Verify bytes and barriers, then measure the final writable tips. Their current highwater summaries live in the loose ledger; they cannot be inside their own fully hashed pack. One HEAD publication selects target semantic roots, per-root placement, measured ledger, original phase and exact global overlay together. Apply positive actual highwater deltas `C` once and retain `R−C`; refuse any disagreement with original bounds or selected finalizer.
6. **Reconcile and clean.** Validate complete current controls, actual bytes and original identity before deleting known dispatch evidence. Cleanup releases only the residual original hold, without recharging C. Retirement separately drops all required ownership/retention edges, retains the original charge in its selected ticket, then requires authenticated unlink/barrier/absence before once-only project credit. An unselected historical corpus, OS free-space estimate or replacement token cannot supply authority.

Final-tip observations occur after finalizer writes, so the finalizer can bind their exact write recipe and extent bounds but cannot invent their future measured charge. The selected post-write ledger authenticates those observations. This is an explicit late measured field, not permission for an arbitrary successor to change the finalizer or original admission. Current final tips require no circular whole-pack self-hash; once subsequently sealed, their immutable charge evidence must be stored elsewhere.

## Smallest next reviewable implementation

Build a **new** pure candidate reader/generator with this top-level contract, keeping all frozen tests intact. The next bounded implementation is the **settled new-coordinate reader only**: emit one settled route-project HEAD with operation v2, resource v2, scalable accounting, conversion-source and independent active/recovery/pinned placements. Explicitly leave the transition gap open. A subsequent separately verified slice adds same-token admission → witnessed birth → payload → selected measured finalizer → atomic publication → cleanup, followed by retirement. Retain independent D19 preservation/audit cases. Use one shared bounded canonical parser and exact capability/coordinate dispatch; publish the actual field table and bytes before describing the result as final-coordinate evidence.

Required coherent negatives are wrong per-pin placement/ownership; caller-invented origin evidence; contradictory shared association identity; reordered/missing tree members; newborn charge substituted after finalizer selection; underreserved finalizer/control coexistence before effects; forged old-base or original reserve; source reappearance or changed destination suffix before cleanup; and credit without all pins/absence proof. Full package validation proves the exact global closure and ledger. A separate read-only recovery entry must validate recovery's own selected semantic/physical closure and necessary authenticated controls after active-only allocations are removed; it does not claim global active closure, writable accounting authority or retirement eligibility in that reduced world. Preserve structural readability versus retention/qualification support, and metadata opening versus Blob audit, as separate results.

Still engineering work: exact new ledger/hold/ticket/pin/evidence schemas, maximum-phase size arithmetic, origin dispatcher vectors, whole-HEAD new-coordinate bytes, measured-finalizer replay/negative evidence, and a consolidated capability/codec table. These are not reasons to change retention or ask for a production policy decision. Permanent names/versions/encoding approval, portable/local custody and qualified lease/storage behavior, actual production deployment, and any distinct unsupported codec upgrade remain explicit review boundaries. This document freezes none of them and claims no new native crash, power-loss or filesystem qualification evidence.
